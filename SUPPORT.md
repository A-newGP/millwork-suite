# Getting help

OpenCabinet is a volunteer project in pre-alpha; there is no software to
support yet. In the meantime:

- **Questions, ideas and millwork know-how:** use
  [GitHub Discussions](../../discussions). English and Spanish are both
  welcome.
- **Documentation problems or concrete proposals:** open an
  [issue](../../issues/new/choose).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never use
  public channels.
- **Conduct concerns:** follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

The project does not offer commercial support. Answers come from volunteers
in their free time, so please be patient.
