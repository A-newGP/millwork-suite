# Trademark policy

> [!NOTE]
> **Draft, pending legal review.** No trademark is registered yet.

The software licenses (GPL, AGPL, MIT, Creative Commons) give you broad
rights over the **code and documentation**. They do **not** grant rights to
the OpenCabinet name, logo or the "official" designation. This policy
governs only how something is *presented*, never how the software is *used*.

## You may, without asking

- Use, study, modify and redistribute the software under its licenses.
- Refer to the project by name to describe it truthfully: "compatible with
  OpenCabinet", "a plugin for OpenCabinet", "based on OpenCabinet".
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, talks and reviews.
- Distribute unmodified official releases under the original name.

## You may not

- Name a modified version, fork, product or service OpenCabinet, or use a
  confusingly similar name or logo.
- Present your work as official, endorsed or verified by the project unless
  it is listed as such in the official plugin registry.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests affiliation where none exists.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that is what free software is for. Please give your fork
a different name and logo, and say clearly that it is based on OpenCabinet.

## Questions

Open a [discussion](../../discussions) if you are unsure whether a use is
allowed.
